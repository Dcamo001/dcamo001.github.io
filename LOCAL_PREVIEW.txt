Why file:/// looks broken in Firefox
-------------------------------------
When you open index.html from the Desktop (file:///...), the browser loads that one file.
Your page also needs styles/style.css, images/, logos/, and other .html files. Firefox often
blocks or mishandles those extra "local file" requests — so you see no styling, broken images,
duplicate mobile/desktop blocks (because the CSS that hides one of them never loads), and
sometimes "Access to the file was denied" when you click a link to another page.

That is normal browser behavior for file:// — it is not a bug in your reverted site files.
